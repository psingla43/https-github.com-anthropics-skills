Accessibility statement

Our commitment

We are committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, and to treating accessibility as part of how the site is built and maintained rather than an afterthought.

The standard we aim for

We aim to align this website with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) at the level required under Ontario's accessibility legislation. [Confirm the current standard and level that applies to your organization before stating a specific version.]

What we have done

Steps taken in building this site include:

  • semantic HTML structure with a single main heading per page and headings in order;
  • landmark regions (header, navigation, main content, and footer) for screen-reader navigation;
  • a "skip to main content" link and keyboard-operable navigation and forms;
  • visible focus outlines so keyboard users can see where they are;
  • form fields with programmatic labels;
  • colour that is not used as the only way to convey meaning, and attention to text contrast;
  • support for reduced-motion preferences; and
  • a layout that adapts to different screen sizes and to enlarged text.

Where we may fall short

Accessibility is ongoing work, and some content or third-party components may not yet meet our goal. If you encounter a barrier, we want to hear about it so we can address it.

Alternate formats

If you need information from this website in a different format, or need help using the site, contact us and we will work with you to provide the information in a way that meets your needs.
